## 1. Who SEIT actually is

SEIT is **not a company or a consumer brand**. It is a **SEBI-registered Infrastructure Investment Trust (InvIT)** listed on the NSE under the ticker **SEITINVIT**.

Plain-language explanation: an InvIT works like a mutual fund, except it holds income-producing infrastructure instead of stocks. SEIT's infrastructure is solar power plants. It collects the revenue those plants earn from selling electricity and passes most of it to investors as regular payouts called distributions. Investors buy and sell "units" on the stock exchange like shares.

## 5. Competitive landscape (InvITs only)

**SEIT is currently the only pure renewable-energy InvIT listed in India.** Its closest historic peer, Virescent Renewable Energy Trust (KKR-sponsored, India's first renewable InvIT), was acquired outright by IndiGrid in August 2023 for up to ₹4,000 crore.

| Trust | Holds | Sponsor | Proximity to SEIT |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IndiGrid** | Transmission + ~1.5 GWp solar + 900 MWh BESS; ~USD 3.7bn assets | KKR | **Closest rival.** Largest power InvIT, AAA, now matches SEIT on solar capacity |
| **Anzen India Energy Yield Plus** | Transmission + some solar generation | Sekura Energy / Edelweiss (EAAA) | Hybrid energy, partial overlap |
| **Powergrid InvIT (PGInvIT)** | Pure power transmission | Power Grid Corp (PSU) | Same yield shelf, no generation |

Wider field competing for the same yield investor: NHIT, IRB InvIT, Cube Highways, Bharat Highways, Capital Infra (roads); Altius Telecom, Data Infrastructure Trust (fibre/telecom).

### The competitive urgency
**Two days before this research, IndiGrid's Managing Director HarsH Shah launched the inaugural episode of "Building Wealth with InvITs"**, a podcast series by the **Bharat InvITs Association** in collaboration with **ET Markets**, explicitly aimed at investor education. IndiGrid's framing on their page:

> "Building confidence in an asset class begins with building a better understanding of it."

IndiGrid is claiming the investor-education position for the whole asset class. The Bharat InvITs Association itself has only **3,730 followers**, so the category's own platform is small and the space is still contestable.

## 7. Regulatory constraints (a genuine differentiator)

SEIT is a SEBI-regulated listed trust, so LinkedIn content is not free-form marketing.

- Public communications, publicity materials and advertisements must stay within the scope of the offer document and be fair and truthful.
- Advertisement material **shall not be misleading and shall not contain anything extraneous to the contents of the offer document**.
- **Where an advertisement contains positive highlights, it must also carry risk factors with equal importance in all aspects, including print size.**
- No advertisement may be issued for units offered via private placement.
- Periodic and continuous disclosure obligations sit under Regulation 23 and Chapter 4 of SEBI's Master Circular for InvITs.

**Practical implication for the proposal:** every post touching performance, yield or distributions needs a compliance gate, and Ilaa Udeshi is that gate. Build a review SLA and a four-week-ahead calendar into the engagement or the content pipeline will stall. Most agencies pitching SEIT will not know these rules exist, which makes this a credibility moat.

*Caveat: the most detailed accessible source is the 2016 public-issue guidelines. Verify current text against sebi.gov.in before quoting specifics to the client.*

## 9. Strategic diagnosis

### Three gaps

1. **Reach.** 2,921 followers against IndiGrid's 77,955 (27×) and their own sponsor Mahindra Susten's 91,197 (31×).
2. **Consistency.** The story stops at the edge of the feed: one sponsor-issued press release since January 2024 filed under "Uncategorized", a careers page inviting people to "Join SEIT" with no role, application or human being on it, and a LinkedIn page still declaring itself "Privately Held" with no tagline, headquarters or specialties.
3. **Ownership.** No communications, brand or content function exists. Investor relations sits with the Compliance Officer. Everything reads like a filing because it is written by the people responsible for filings.

### The insight that reframes it

**SEIT's content already works. Their distribution doesn't.**

SEIT averages ~2.6% engagement. IndiGrid averages ~0.14%. Post for post, SEIT earns roughly **19× more attention per follower** than the market leader. Their best-performing post is a human fireside chat, which outperformed their own quarterly results post.

So the pitch is not "your content is bad." It is "you have proven content and no audience." That diagnosis is more accurate, more flattering, and points to a different and more valuable scope: audience building, executive amplification and employee advocacy rather than a content overhaul.

Caveat to hold honestly: part of that engagement is manufactured by tagging thirteen colleagues in every post, and there are zero comments across all posts.

### The platform idea

# Make the megawatts visible.

Retail investors ignore InvITs because infrastructure feels abstract. SEIT's is not abstract. Robotic cleaners across 700 MW, 79 million litres of water saved annually, 2,457 GWh on the grid, 1.68 million tonnes of CO₂ avoided, eight plants across five states, independently assured. All of it currently lives inside PDFs.

One idea, two audiences: **the investor story and the people story are the same story told to different rooms.**

## 10. Solution architecture

### Pillar one — the investor story
| Format | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Distribution Note** | Every quarter, ₹3.14 per unit becomes a designed, plain-English explanation of what unitholders earned and why |

| **The Proof Series** | AAA/Stable, 7.45× water positive, 1.68 mn tCO₂e avoided, treated as headlines rather than footnotes |

Note: none of these require new corporate disclosure. All the material already exists in filings and the sustainability report. Only the form changes. This matters enormously to a compliance-led room.

### Pillar two — the people story
| Format | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Fireside, properly made** | #SEITFiresidechats is their best content but currently a text recap. Turn it into a filmed, designed, recurring property with feed-native clips |
| **Give the culture a face** | Life at SEIT makes four claims about culture and shows nobody living them. Shoot and write those stories with their own 44 people, then run them as a series that feeds the site |
| **The lean team advantage** | At 44 people a new joiner sits a desk from the decisions. Most renewable employers cannot offer that. Make it a recruiting argument |
| **Grow. Perform. Sustain.** | They have already written a sharp EVP. It has never left the website |

### The amplification engine (how a 27× gap actually closes)
1. **Executive voices.** The CEO appears at World Bank and IFC roundtables and says nothing on his own feed. One leader posting weekly outruns a company page, with no media spend.
2. **Employee advocacy.** Tagging the same thirteen colleagues is a workaround, not advocacy. Give 44 people something they want to share.
3. **Sponsor gravity.** Mahindra Susten has 91,197 followers; Ontario Teachers' is a global institution. Neither currently amplifies SEIT. A coordinated calendar is free reach they already own.

### How it ships
- 12 to 14 posts a month across both pillars
- One filmed property per quarter, starting with the fireside series
- An executive ghostwriting track for the CEO plus one other voice
- Always-on community management (their posts currently get reactions and no replies)
- Calendar built four weeks ahead so compliance review never becomes the bottleneck
- Every post touching performance, yield or distributions routes through a named review gate with Ilaa Udeshi, with agreed turnaround and escalation, logged

| Phase | Work | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Days 1-30 — Foundation** | Page rebuilt, metadata corrected, positioning line written, Life at SEIT given real faces and a way to apply, compliance workflow agreed, 90-day calendar approved | SEIT's page finally states what SEIT is |
| **Days 31-60 — Activation** | Both pillars live, first fireside filmed, career stories shot, executive track begins, Inside the Asset visits a plant | A feed with a recognisable point of view, publishing twice a week |
| **Days 61-90 — Compound** | Employee advocacy switched on, sponsor amplification coordinated, investor education series launches | Growth stops depending on the agency posting |
